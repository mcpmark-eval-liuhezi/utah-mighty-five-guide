## What it is

A red-rock landscape of over 2,000 natural stone arches, hundreds of soaring pinnacles, massive fins, and giant balanced rocks. It's part of the Colorado Plateau "high desert," so expect big temperature swings — sometimes 40 °F in a single day. Winter days run 30–50 °F with nights from 0–20 °F.

The park itself is open 24 hours a day, year-round.

## Campgrounds & amenities

### Devils Garden Campground — the only campground in the park

- **Sites:** 51 total (49 individual + 2 group); **18 miles inside the park entrance**
- **Fee:** $25/night standard site (up to 10 people, 2 vehicles)
- **Amenities:** drinking water, picnic tables, grills, fire pits, pit-style **and** flush toilets (year-round), trash/recycling, amphitheater, dump station, food storage lockers, camp host onsite, firewood for sale (at the visitor center bookstore only — *not* at the campground), cell reception
- **RVs:** allowed, up to 40 ft; paved roads, all vehicles OK
- **Reservations — critical for our dates:** reservable up to 6 months ahead for nights **March 1 – October 31**. **Between November 1 and February 28 all sites are first-come, first-served.** Winter campers are told to download the **Recreation.gov app before arriving** to use for payment.
- **Regulations worth knowing:** quiet hours 10 pm–8 am, checkout 10 am, generators only 8–10 am and 4–8 pm, fires only in fire pits/grills, burning collected wood prohibited (buy firewood coupons), 10-person limit per site.

**Our pick: Devils Garden** — it's the only option, but it's a good one: flush toilets, drinking water, and you wake up at the trailhead for the Devils Garden arch hike. From Nov 1 it's first-come, first-served, so arrive early in the day (the park recommends entering before 8 am anyway to dodge entrance lines).

## Notes from the park website (checked late October 2026)

1. **No timed-entry reservation is required to enter Arches** — but the park does **temporarily delay entries** when the entrance line backs up to the highway, and between March and October entrance waits can exceed 60 minutes. November is off-peak, so this is unlikely to affect us badly. The [park homepage](https://www.nps.gov/arch/index.htm) is currently promoting a **new free Moab-to-Arches shuttle** to the visitor center and select park destinations as an alternative to driving in. *(Source: nps.gov/arch — homepage and Alerts & Conditions, last updated Mar 18, 2026.)*

2. **Road and campground status:** the main (paved) park road is open; unpaved side roads may require 4WD or be impassable after storms. Campground reservations run March–October, and "in winter, sites are first-come, first-served" — which is exactly our situation from Nov 1. *(Source: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)*

3. **Crowd guidance:** parking at Devils Garden, Delicate Arch, and The Windows is usually full much of the day in peak season; enter early morning or late afternoon for cooler temps and better light. *(Source: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/traffic.htm)*
