## What it is

The largest concentration of **hoodoos** (irregular rock columns) on Earth, perched on a high plateau at the top of the Grand Staircase. Because of the elevation (8,000–9,000 ft), the park also has dark skies, conifer forests, and genuinely cold shoulder-season weather — **from October to May, temperatures fall below freezing nearly every night.**

## Campgrounds & amenities

### North Campground

- **Sites:** 100 across 4 loops (A, B, C, D); **across the road from the visitor center**
- **Fee:** $30/night (tent or RV site)
- **Amenities:** flush toilets (year-round), potable water (seasonal), picnic tables, fire rings, trash/recycling, food storage lockers, camp host, cell reception; **dump station and potable water near the campground are summer-only**
- **Loops:** **A & B are for RV campers; C & D are tent loops and are closed in freezing temperatures.**
- **Seasonal openings that matter for us:** "Typically, from **October 5 – December 31, only Loops A & B are open.**" From Jan 1–Apr 15 only Loop A is open.
- **Reservations:** open year-round; 96 of 100 sites reservable — see go.nps.gov/BryceCamping
- **Regulations:** max 6 people/site (6 adults), 3 tents, 2 cars (or 1 RV + tow vehicle); quiet hours 10 pm–8 am; check-in/out 11:30 am; no hammocks hung from trees; no portable showers or toilets.

### Sunset Campground

- **Sites:** 99 in 3 loops (A primarily RV; B & C tent-only); ~1.5 miles south of the visitor center, west of Sunset Point, with a shuttle stop at its entrance
- **Fee:** posted per-site nightly fee
- **Amenities:** picnic tables, fire rings, flush toilets, potable water near the dump station (summer only), trash/recycling
- **⚠️ Sunset Campground is closed in winter** — per its NPS record, "Sunset Campground is closed in winter," and its operating window is the summer season. **Not available for our trip.**

**Our pick: North Campground, and honestly, bring a Plan B.** In early November only Loops A & B are open and the tent loops (C & D) are shut for freezing weather, while Sunset is closed outright. If we're tent camping in below-freezing nights at 8,000 ft, we want a serious cold-weather bag, and it's worth pricing a motel in Bryce Canyon City or Tropic as a backup.

This also means no oversized-vehicle parking restrictions in the Bryce Amphitheater (those run April–October during shuttle hours), and the general guidance to "leave your vehicle at your campsite and ride the shuttle" doesn't apply. *(Source: https://www.nps.gov/brca/index.htm — homepage feature "2026 Shuttle Season.")*
