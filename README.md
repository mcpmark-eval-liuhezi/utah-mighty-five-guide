# Utah's Mighty Five — November 2026 Trip Guide

Two weeks, one rental car, five national parks. Fly into Salt Lake City, drive a big loop, camp most nights, fly home.

All facts below come from the National Park Service's own records (park details, visitor centers, campgrounds, and the event calendar) plus each park's official website for current, practical logistics. Checked **late October 2026**.

## The Mighty Five — verified against official NPS records

Yes, your list is right. All five are officially designated **National Park** in **Utah (UT)**:

| Park | Park code | State | Entrance fee (private vehicle) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Arches National Park](arches.md) | `arch` | UT | $30 / 7 days |
| [Bryce Canyon National Park](bryce-canyon.md) | `brca` | UT | $35 |
| [Canyonlands National Park](canyonlands.md) | `cany` | UT | $30 / 7 days |
| [Capitol Reef National Park](capitol-reef.md) | `care` | UT | $20 |
| [Zion National Park](zion.md) | `zion` | UT | $35 / 1–7 days |

**Money tip:** an *America the Beautiful* annual pass ($80) covers entry to all five parks for the year — cheaper than paying the five entrance fees separately (~$140). Arches + Canyonlands also share a $55 local annual pass.

**Weather reality check for November:** Bryce sits above 8,000 ft and drops below freezing nearly every night from October through May; the lower-elevation parks (Arches, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef, Zion Canyon) run milder days (highs often 50s–60s °F) with cold nights. Pack for both.

## Suggested driving order — a two-week loop out of Salt Lake City

**SLC → Moab (Arches + Canyonlands) → Capitol Reef → Bryce Canyon → Zion → SLC**

Roughly 1,100 miles with no backtracking. The logic:

- **Moab first** — it's the farthest hub from SLC but lets you knock out two parks (Arches + Canyonlands) from a single base with showers, groceries, and laundry.
- **Capitol Reef sits on the natural line** between Moab and Bryce — no detour required, and it's the quietest of the five.
- **UT-12 from Torrey to Bryce** is one of the most scenic drives in the country and is the road you'd take anyway.
- **Zion last** — it's the closest of the five to I-15, so the final run back to SLC is the easiest big drive of the trip (~4.5 hrs).

## The three website notes that matter most

1. **Zion Canyon Scenic Drive is closed to private cars during shuttle season — and the shuttle is still running during our dates.** Per the [Zion Canyon Shuttle System page](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-shuttle-system.htm): from **October 25 to November 28, 2026**, the first shuttle leaves the visitor center at **7:00 am**, the last leaves the visitor center at **5:00 pm**, and the last shuttle out of the canyon (Temple of Sinawava) is **6:15 pm**. No permit, ticket, or reservation is needed to enter the park or ride the shuttle — but you *cannot* drive the scenic road, even before or after shuttle hours. You *can* still drive the Zion–Mt. Carmel Highway (SR-9, which crosses the park), Kolob Terrace Road, and Kolob Canyons Scenic Drive. **Don't plan to catch the last shuttle of the day.**

2. **Arches' Devils Garden campground flips to first-come, first-served on November 1 — the day we land.** Between **November 1 and February 28 there are no reservations**; NPS says to download the Recreation.gov app *before arriving* to pay. Meanwhile **Zion's Watchman campground is reservation-only year-round** (no FCFS sites) and books up to six months ahead — we're inside that window now, so **book Watchman immediately**. (Sources: [Arches camping](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/camping.htm), [Watchman](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/watchman-campground.htm).)

3. **Bryce Canyon's shuttle season ended October 18 — we drive ourselves.** More importantly, at Bryce only **Loops A & B of North Campground are open from October 5–December 31**; the tent-oriented Loops C & D are closed in freezing temperatures, and **Sunset Campground is closed for winter entirely** ([North Campground](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/northcampground.htm)). November nights at 8,000+ ft will be below freezing — bring a cold-rated bag and seriously consider a lodge/ motel backup for the Bryce night.

### Honorable mentions

- **Canyonlands:** the western side of the **White Rim Road is closed and impassable** (rockfall/washouts; alert posted Oct 15, 2026, closed through at least Oct 20). Only relevant if we get 4WD ambitions.
- **Capitol Reef:** the **Gifford House** (pies and pioneer goods) is open daily 9 am–4:30 pm and doesn't close for the season until **Nov 22** — we make it. Apple harvest is running (apples $2/lb as of the Oct 18 orchard update).
- **Zion:** **Angels Landing requires a permit** for all hikers; the **Watchman Trail is closed** pending repairs; **toxic cyanobacteria** advisories mean don't submerge your head in or drink river water.
- **Arches:** no timed-entry system is currently in place, but on very busy days the entrance can temporarily close when the line backs up to the highway — enter early. A **new free Moab-to-Arches shuttle** is being promoted on the park homepage as an alternative to driving in.
